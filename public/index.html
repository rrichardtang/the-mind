      How to play

      • The deck is 1 to 100. On level N everyone is dealt N cards.
      • Together you must play every card into one pile in ascending order.
      • No talking, no hinting, no counting out loud. Just feel the timing.
      • Play a card while someone still holds a lower one and you lose a life. Every lower card is discarded, and everyone involved gets called out.
      • A shuriken needs everyone to agree. Each player then discards their lowest card face up.
      • Clearing certain levels earns a bonus life or shuriken.
      • On a timed run each level gives you a set number of seconds per card dealt. Let the clock reach zero and the whole run is over. The clock pauses while anyone is offline.
      • Lives at zero ends the run. Clear the final level and you have beaten The Mind.
